# Grand Palm Hotel Lagos — the rebrand

The INFINEON Enterprise Platform is now presented to guests as **Grand Palm Hotel Lagos**, a
fictional 50-room five-star property on Lagos Island. This is a deliberate product shift:
the platform's surface that the public sees (landing, booking, auth, confirmation) now reads
as a luxury hospitality brand, not a B2B software pitch.

## Visual direction

- **Lagoonfront, gold-on-emerald.** The default theme (Emerald Gold) anchors the brand:
  cream-and-gold type on a deep emerald ground, with a glowy gold accent for CTAs.
- **Real hotel imagery.** Every public page uses Unsplash photography of suites, lagoons,
  pools, spas, lobbies, and restaurants. No icons-as-illustrations.
- **Editorial typography.** Serif display face for headlines (Georgia now, swapping in
  Didot / Songti SC for the other themes), tight letter-spacing for caps eyebrows.
- **Soft horizontal pace.** Sections alternate between "lagoon-tinted" (bg-brand-900/30) and
  full-bleed photo bands, with a generous vertical rhythm.

## Why this matters

The INFINEON platform has 13 internal modules — housekeeping, finance, CRM, etc. — none of
which a guest should see. By putting the property first (Grand Palm) and the platform second
(INFINEON · Enterprise), we keep the B2B operators as the real audience while making the
public surface feel like a real hotel. This unlocks the booking funnel as a showcase of the
platform's actual capability: live inventory, real-time availability, payment, and the
back-of-house pipeline that operators can demo in the same session.
