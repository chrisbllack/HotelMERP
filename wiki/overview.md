INFINEON is an all-encompassing hospitality ERP / PMS — front desk, lodging, F&B, finance, workforce, distribution, payments, events, devices, intelligence, CRM, supply chain, platform. Demo seeded against Grand Palm Hotel Lagos (50 rooms). Built on Vite + React 19 + TypeScript + Tailwind v4 with a Convex backend.

Two surfaces:
- **Staff console** (protected) — 18 module pages
- **Public booking site** — landing, room search, guest details, confirmation, lookup

The product is being extended to be world-class with inspiration from US (Four Seasons, Park Hyatt), India (Taj, Oberoi), Nigeria (Eko Atlantic, coastal resorts), China (Mandarin Oriental, Peninsula), modern boutique (W, Ace), and tropical resorts (Bawah, Soneva).
